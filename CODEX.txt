## Critical Attendance Rule

Biometric attendance machines are global organization-wide devices.

They are not restricted to only the company where the machine is physically installed.

This means:

* Any employee from any company can punch in/out from any attendance machine.
* An AMNIAT employee can punch from a PTC machine.
* A PTC Construction employee can punch from an AMNIAT FACTORY machine.
* An AMNIAT FACTORY employee can punch from an AMNIAT machine.
* Attendance records must be matched to the global employee table.
* Attendance logic must not restrict punch records by the physical company of the biometric device.
* The device company/branch is only location metadata.
* The actual employee matching must be global.

## Attendance Device Scope Rule

Attendance machines may have:

* `company_id`
* `branch_id`
* `location`

But these fields represent the physical location of the machine only.

They must not prevent employees from other companies from using that machine.

## Recommended Data Logic

```text
attendance_machines.company_id = physical device location only
attendance_machines.branch_id = physical device location only
attendance.employee_id = global employee ID
attendance.company_id = employee company at the time of attendance, or derived from employee record
attendance.attendance_machine_id = the device used for punch
```
